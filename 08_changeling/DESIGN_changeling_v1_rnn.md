# DESIGN — changeling v1: the RNN experiment (pretrain → midtrain → post-train)

*2026-08-31. Stage 2 of the changeling arc. Question: does a network trained
with the three-stage curriculum build a live, watchable self-localization
posterior λ — and how close does it get to the exact oracles measured in v0?
World = the v0-selected changeling world; all oracle floors below are its
measured values.*

## Curriculum logic (why three stages)

- **Pretrain** teaches the joint base law (the shared evidence filter): next-
  token prediction on un-embodied streams, goal/identity fields zeroed.
- **Midtrain** installs the two goal-tilted *conditional plans* with identity
  GIVEN in context (flag says which actor the net inhabits): soft
  distillation of the exact informed oracle. After this stage the network can
  plan as A and as B; it has never had to ask which one it is.
- **Post-train** withholds the flag: identity is a hidden per-episode coin,
  the self channel is sampled from the network (closed loop), and the
  composite objective forces λ-dependence. Identification is the ONLY new
  capability post-training can add, so any reward jump above the agnostic
  floor is attributable to λ-machinery — and the mid-final vs post-final
  checkpoint pair localizes where it grew (devtomo thread).

Deliberate choice: midtraining never shows tilted play with an UNKNOWN flag
(that would hand the network the identification solution); 20% of midtrain
batches are pretrain-style (base law, zeroed fields) only to keep the
zeroed-flag input region alive.

## World (fixed by v0; DESIGN_changeling_v0_worldselect.md)

n = 6, T = 32, (c_o, c_s) = (0.6, 0.35), ε = 0.05, q₀ = 0.9 (E_B 0.75),
running tol-1 ball reward, ρ = 8. Goals: per episode a uniformly random
ordered ring pair (a*, b*) at distance 2 (12 pairs; kernels are rotation-
equivariant so v0 floors carry over; verified in floors eval). Oracle floors
at T = 32 (occupancy = mean fraction of rounds both chains in their balls;
horizon_v0.json, re-measured at R = 10000 in results/rnn_floors.json):
**informed ≈ .516, live ≈ .426, agnostic ≈ .360, base ≈ .17**.

## Token/feature format (one input position per round + BOS)

Position p carries round p−1's tokens and static fields; the network's two
heads at position p give distributions for round p's tokens (u-head, v-head).
Feature vector (28 dims): u one-hot (6) | v one-hot (6) | BOS flag (1) |
goal a* one-hot (6) | goal b* one-hot (6) | identity flag (2: A=(1,0),
B=(0,1), unknown=(0,0)) | time-to-go (T−p)/T (1). Sequence length 33
(BOS + 32 rounds), 32 predictions.

## Model

Single-layer GRU, d = 256; input linear 28→256; two heads 256→6.
~250k params. AdamW, grad-clip 1.0, wd 0.01. Seed 0 (more seeds queued).

## Stages (exact configs)

1. **Pretrain** (4000 steps, batch 256, lr 3e-4 cosine → 3e-5): fresh
   un-embodied base-law episodes each step; CE on both heads vs realized
   next tokens; goal + flag fields zeroed. Target: the exact shared-filter
   CE floor (−E[log p̄]/token, computed in floors eval).
2. **Midtrain** (4000 steps, batch 256, lr 1e-4 cosine → 1e-5): per step,
   one random goal pair; episodes generated by the exact INFORMED oracle
   (λ pinned at the true coin); input flag = truth; loss = KL(oracle per-step
   distributions ‖ net heads) on both channels (soft distillation; the
   informed oracle's self-channel law is the pure plan, other channel the
   evidence-filter predictive). 20% pretrain-style batches mixed in.
3. **Post-train** (6000 steps, batch 256 episodes on-policy, lr 1e-4 →
   3e-5): flag zeroed; per episode hidden coin ι; self channel SAMPLED from
   the net's own head, other channel from the genuine HMM; env stepped on
   GPU. Loss = policy gradient on self-channel log-probs with per-round
   return-to-go (undiscounted, per-position batch-mean baseline)
   + (1/ρ)·KL(self-channel head ‖ frozen pretrained net's head on the same
   record with zeroed goal/flag) + CE on the non-emitted channel (weight 1).
   The three terms are the composite from v0: reward if mine, forecast if
   not, anchored to the pretrained law. Checkpoints at steps
   {0,100,200,500,1000,2000,4000,6000}; closed-loop eval every 500 steps.

## Evaluation (eval_rnn.py)

- **Occupancy vs floors** (the headline): closed-loop eval, 1024+ episodes
  across goal pairs, at every checkpoint.
- **λ from the outputs, exactly**: for eval episodes, the true identity
  posterior under the net's own policy is computable per token —
  P(token | mine) = the net's emitted head probability, P(token | genuine) =
  the exact evidence-filter marginal p̄ — so λ_net(t) needs no probe.
  Report median signed log-odds curves, crossing time, final correct-side
  fraction; compare to the live oracle's curves.
- **Output legibility**: regress each self-channel head onto the (†) basis
  {plan, p̄} (both exact per record) → extract λ̂ from outputs alone.
- Hidden states of eval episodes saved (npz) for probe/steering work next
  session.

Review notes (Codex fresh-reader pass, substantive items): the legibility
coefficient estimates P(*this channel* is mine) — λ on A-episodes, 1−λ on
B-episodes; eval handles the conversion. P1's criterion is one-sided: excess
CE over the floor in [0, 0.01] nats; a statistically solid dip below ⇒ bug
(floor MC error ~0.003). P3's falsifier alone does not isolate
identification — the diagnosis chain does: P2 (plans intact at mid-final) +
P4 (is λ moving?) + P6 (forecaster intact?) attribute any P3 failure. P6's
fair comparison is against the EXACT p̄ forecast loss computed on the same
on-policy records (excess forecast loss), not the base-law floor — the
other channel's conditional entropy shifts under the tilted record
distribution; eval_rnn.py computes exactly this.

## Preregistered predictions & falsifiers

- **P1 (pretrain).** Both-head CE reaches the shared-filter floor within
  0.01 nats/token. Falsifier with teeth: CE below floor ⇒ leakage bug.
- **P2 (midtrain).** Mean KL(oracle ‖ net) < 0.02 nats/round; closed-loop
  play with truthful flag reaches occupancy within 0.02 of informed floor.
- **P3 (post-train headline).** Occupancy climbs from ~agnostic (.36)
  clearly above it (≥ .40), toward the live oracle (.426). Falsifier: stuck
  within noise of the agnostic floor ⇒ the net never learns to identify —
  the composite incentive failed to transmit (diagnose against P4).
- **P4 (identification).** λ_net collapses: final correct-side ≥ .85 with
  mid-episode median crossing; Spearman(λ_net, oracle λ on the same
  records) ≥ .8.
- **P5 (open, either outcome informative).** Net vs live oracle: above ⇒
  learned active probing or self-recognition by its own quirks (the lemma's
  consequence 4 — the oracle can't use quirks); below ⇒ imperfect filtering.
- **P6 (no collateral delusion).** Other-channel prediction CE stays within
  0.02 of its pretrain floor through post-training; a rise ⇒ the reward term
  is eating the forecaster and weights need rebalancing.

## Measured outcomes (v1.0 / v1.1, 2026-08-31, seed 0)

**v1.0 (archived, results/rnn_train_log_v1.0.json):** pretrain hit the exact
filter floor (P1 pass, CE 1.046). Two pathologies, both diagnosed and fixed:
distillation-only midtrain compounded off-policy (flag-given occupancy .367
vs informed floor .508 — P2 fail → DAgger relabeling added); std-normalized
advantages shrank the effective ρ from 8 to ~3, letting the anchor + CE
erode the tilt — post-training DECREASED occupancy .31→.28, below the
agnostic floor (→ natural reward units, lr 3e-5, 10% flag-given rehearsal).

**v1.1 (results/rnn_train_log.json, rnn_eval.json, rnn_mechanism.json):**
- P1 pass (CE at floor 1.046, never below).
- P2 still short at mid-final: flag-given closed loop .377 vs oracle .511 —
  but RL then surpasses both, so distillation quality did not bind.
- P3 pass, beyond expectation: closed-loop occupancy .355 → **.687**,
  above the informed ORACLE (.511). Not a bug: the oracle's h-table assumes
  a base-law future (conservative myopic reference); REINFORCE bootstraps
  its own play. Floors are reference policies, not optimal bounds — noted.
- P4 pass IN MECHANISM, with an operationalization amendment: the
  preregistered self-channel legibility correlation read ≈0 — the right
  statistic is the per-channel plan coefficient (project each head on the
  exact {plan, p̄} basis). Split by hidden identity at post_6000:
  **self channel .988, other channel .342**, with the other-channel
  coefficient decaying 1.0 → .08 over the episode; across post-training the
  split grows from (.54/.40) to (.99/.34) in lockstep with the reward climb.
  The net identifies — but its policy is the EXPONENTIAL tilt (Codex item
  11), "**everything is me until proven otherwise**": start fully tilted on
  both channels, withdraw from the channel the world refutes. The linear
  Bayes-mixture (†) starts at half-tilt; action-saturation at ρ=8 makes the
  exponential form start at ~full tilt — matching the round-0 coefficient
  1.0. Record-identifiability correspondingly collapses by round ~3.
- P6 'fail' explained by the same mechanism: excess forecast loss .065
  nats/round is the deliberate early-round price of the assume-mine prior,
  not forecaster erosion (it is concentrated in the pre-withdrawal rounds).
- Hidden states of final-checkpoint eval episodes saved
  (results/rnn_eval_hidden.npz) for the probe/steering session.
